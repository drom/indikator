Skin

The IN27D device has 176x264 eInk Black/White screen.

Scale skin

Screen can be logically divided in two vertical tiles (Left and Right).

Two mechanical needles are moving over dedicated screen area.

Specific scale has to be rendered under the needle depending on current context.

Scale skin can be described using JavaScript plugin.
